How it works

01

We index Pons

Every new token from the Pons launchpad on Robinhood Chain lands in Pronoia within seconds: market cap, volume, holders, bonding curve, dev history — read straight from the chain.

02

We score 13 metrics

Liquidity, holder concentration, dev and sniper share, buy pressure, wash trading, socials, the creator's past launches — every metric gets a score.

03

AI gives a verdict

The model reads all metrics together, explains the upside and the risks, and tells you: enter, wait or skip — with entry, stop-loss and target levels.

Pronoia (Greek πρόνοια — “foresight”) is an AI analyst for the Pons launchpad on Robinhood Chain. We pull on-chain data for every launch, score each token across 13 metrics and give a verdict: enter, wait or skip.

Not financial advice. Memecoins are extremely volatile — you make your own decisions.
