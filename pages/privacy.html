Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Short version: Pronoia has no accounts, no cookies and no trackers. We don't ask for your name, email or wallet, and we don't sell data. The token data we show comes from a public blockchain.

1. Who we are

Pronoia (“we”, “us”) runs this website, which analyzes tokens launched on the Pons launchpad on Robinhood Chain. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the site.

2. What we collect

We do not collect personal data on purpose. There is no sign-up, no login, no newsletter form and no wallet connection.

Like every website, our server receives technical request data when your browser loads a page: your IP address, the page or API address requested, the time, and your browser's user-agent string.

  • Rate limiting. When you request an AI review, we keep your IP address in server memory for about one minute to limit how many reviews one visitor can request. It is not written to disk and is gone when the server restarts.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider (Railway) may record request metadata in its platform logs for operations and security, under its own retention policy.

3. Cookies and local storage

We do not set cookies and do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

4. Blockchain data

Everything we show about tokens — prices, holders, trades, creator addresses and past launches — is read from Robinhood Chain, a public blockchain. This data is public by nature. Wallet addresses can appear on our pages as part of token analytics; we do not link them to real-world identities.

Links to other sites (Pons, block explorers, X, Telegram, project websites) take you outside Pronoia. Their own privacy policies apply there.

6. How long we keep data

Rate-limit entries: about one minute, in memory only. AI reviews of a token are cached for a few minutes and contain no data about you. Hosting logs: according to Railway's retention.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data. Because we don't keep personal data beyond short-lived technical records, there is usually nothing stored about you that we could return or delete — but you can contact us and we will check.

8. Children

Pronoia is not intended for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If we change how we handle data — for example if we add accounts or analytics — we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: reach us through the official channels linked in the site footer.
